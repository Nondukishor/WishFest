Privacy Policy for WishFest
Effective date: 24 October 2026. App: WishFest (com.nipu.wishfest). Privacy contact: pro.nipu@gmail.com.

Your cards and photos
WishFest stores drafts, message text, recipient names, sticker choices, and selected photos in app-private storage on your device. It does not operate an account system, cloud card service, advertising service, or developer-run analytics backend. A system file chooser provides access to files you select; the app does not request broad photo-library permission.

Optional on-device AI
On supported devices, WishFest can use Google ML Kit and Android AICore to generate wishes locally. Greeting inputs and generated text are processed on-device. If AI is unavailable, offline greeting templates are used. Google services may access the network for service configuration and maintenance.

Google SDK diagnostics
Google documents that ML Kit collects device and app information, identifiers, performance and event diagnostics, input/output sizes, and configured languages for diagnostics and usage analytics. Google states this data is encrypted in transit. See https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure and https://policies.google.com/privacy. WishFest does not control retention of Google-managed diagnostics.

Saving and sharing
Saving creates an image in Pictures/WishFest on your device. Sharing grants the app you choose access to the exported image through Android. The receiving app handles the image under its own privacy policy. Shared copies and saved images remain until you or the recipient delete them.

Retention and deletion
Drafts remain until you delete them in My cards, clear app storage, or uninstall WishFest. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes app-private drafts and imported photos. Deleting a draft removes its project folder; assets referenced by duplicated drafts can remain with the original project. Exports in Photos and copies in other apps are separate. This release excludes private app data from Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup.

Children and contacts
WishFest has no public profiles, accounts, social feed, or developer messaging service. If you email support, your email address and the information you choose to include are used to respond to your request. Do not include sensitive personal information in a support message.

Questions and updates
Contact pro.nipu@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests concerning information you have sent to support. Material changes to these practices will be reflected in an updated policy.
