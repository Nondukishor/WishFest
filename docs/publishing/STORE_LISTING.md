# WishFest — Google Play listing draft

App name: WishFest: Greeting Cards

Short description:
Create festive cards with personal messages, stickers, photos and lovely frames.

Full description:
Make a personal greeting for someone special with WishFest.

Choose a festive template or start a new card. Write your own message, personalize the colors and fonts, and add emoji stickers or a background photo. Give your card a finishing touch with a decorative frame.

CREATE YOUR WAY
• Find templates by occasion or search for a wish.
• Edit messages, colors, fonts and alignment.
• Add emoji stickers, then move, resize or rotate them.
• Explore floral, heart, art deco, ribbon and other creative frames.
• Celebrate Hindu festivals with diya lamps, Om, marigold toran, lotus and rangoli frames.
• Try coordinated Blossom, Midnight and Golden styles.

WISHES FOR EVERY MOMENT
Personalize festive greetings in English, Bengali, Hindi, Tamil, Arabic or German. On supported devices, optional on-device AI can help draft a wish. If AI is unavailable, WishFest uses ready-written offline greetings. You can edit the result before sharing.

SAVE AND SHARE
Keep your drafts in My cards, save a card to Photos, or share it using your favorite app. Choose square, portrait or story output. Cards include a small “Created with WishFest” signature.

Card editing and offline greetings work without an internet connection. Google services used for optional AI may communicate for configuration and diagnostics. No account is required.

Requires Android 10 or later on a supported 64-bit device. On-device AI availability varies by device.
